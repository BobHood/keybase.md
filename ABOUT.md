## 🔑 What is Keybase?

[Keybase](https://keybase.io) is a platform for cryptographic identity verification. It allows you to publicly and verifiably prove that you own accounts across multiple platforms — GitHub, Twitter, Reddit, and more — by signing a cryptographic proof with your personal key.

This means anyone can independently verify that the person behind `BobHood` on GitHub is the same person as `blackknight` on Keybase, without having to take anyone's word for it.
